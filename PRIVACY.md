# Democracydot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026

Democracydot ("we", "us") is an AI-supported civic technology app that helps people understand Tasmanian Parliament Bills and take part in the legislative process. The app is operated by Madeleine Ogilvie.

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we handle

**Information you type into the app**

- **Bill details and questions.** Bill names, summaries, background, policy context, and questions you ask Billbow. These are held only for your current browsing session and are not saved by us.
- **Uploaded documents.** PDFs, text, and Markdown files you upload. They are read in your session and are not saved by us.
- **Essential reading links.** Titles and web addresses you add. They are held only for your current session.
- **Comments.** Feedback you submit on the Democratic Engagement page. Comments are **anonymous**: we do not ask for your name or contact details. We save the comment text, the Bill name you entered, and the time of submission so the app owner can review them. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a comment.

**Sign-up details (earlier versions only)**

To unlock Bill information downloads, the app asks for a name and mobile number. For people who provide them, we hold the name, mobile number, the time they gave permission, and a shortened code identifying the browser used. The details are stored by Democracydot and are not verified. We use these details only to send the updates you agreed to receive.

**Information from official sources**

When you search for a Bill, the app retrieves public Bill records and PDFs from the Parliament of Tasmania and the Tasmanian legislation website. This is public information, not information about you.

**Technical information**

The app does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Streamlit, our app framework, and our hosting provider may process technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the service and keep it secure.

## 2. How we use information

- To retrieve and display Bill documents and generate plain-language summaries, answers, comparisons, and the Democracy Index.
- To let the app owner review anonymous comments.
- To send updates to people who signed up and gave permission.
- To keep the app secure and working.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. AI processing and OpenAI

When you ask for an AI feature (a summary, a Billbow answer, a proposed amendment, a document comparison, or the public discussion summary), the relevant text is sent to OpenAI to produce the result. This can include Bill text, text you typed or uploaded, and your question. It is sent only when you request an AI feature.

OpenAI processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy, available at openai.com/policies. Do not enter or upload personal, confidential, or sensitive information, or material you are not authorised to share.

AI outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate. They are general information only, not legal advice or an official parliamentary assessment. The Democracy Index is an estimate, not a measure of democratic legitimacy.

## 4. Sharing

We share information only with:

- **OpenAI**, as described above, to provide AI features.
- **Our hosting and infrastructure providers**, who store and run the app.
- **Authorities or other parties**, where the law requires it.

The app links to third-party sites, including Facebook, LinkedIn, and the Parliament of Tasmania. Their privacy practices are their own. Checking the box that says you follow a Facebook page is a declaration made in the app; we do not receive information from Facebook about you.

## 5. Storage, security, and retention

Comments and any sign-up details are stored in a database on our hosting service. The database file is access-restricted and is viewable only through a password-protected owner page. Session information is discarded when you close the app.

We keep comments for as long as they are useful for reviewing community feedback, and we may delete them at any time. We keep sign-up details until you ask us to remove them.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information you submit.

Your information may be processed on servers outside Australia, including by OpenAI in the United States.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete sign-up details we hold about you by contacting us. Because comments are anonymous, we may not be able to identify which comment is yours.

You can stop receiving updates at any time by telling us, and we will remove you. You may also ask about how we handle your personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and any other Australian privacy law that applies. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

## 7. Children

The app is intended for people able to take part in public civic discussion and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be noted in the app.
